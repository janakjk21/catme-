# Third-party audio sources

The following individual sounds are used in the HomeRoom prototype. Both source pages mark these files CC0.

- `Resources/CatMeAudio/amb_morning.ogg` — Kresiek The Furry, [AMB Morning Sounds (Perfect Loop)](https://opengameart.org/content/amb-morning-sounds-perfect-loop), CC0.
- `Resources/CatMeAudio/cat_mewfood.wav` — Kerzoven, [Cat Purr & Meow](https://opengameart.org/content/cat-purr-meow), CC0.
- `Resources/CatMeAudio/BallToy/ball_squeak_01.wav` and `ball_squeak_02.wav` — bart, [Squeak toy sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/squeak-toy-sounds), CC0.

The original `Assets/CatMe/Audio/cat-meow.mp3` and `cat-purr.mp3` remain project assets and were not replaced.
