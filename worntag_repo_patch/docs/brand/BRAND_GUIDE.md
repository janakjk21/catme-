# WornTag Brand Guide

## Brand idea

WornTag is not positioned as an AI-to-3D utility. The emotional promise is recognition and presence: **not a dog or a cat, but your dog or your cat.**

Primary brand line: **Closer than a photo.**

Product description: **Turn a favourite photo of your dog or cat into a personalised 3D companion you can see, call and play with.**

## Brand behaviour

- Recognition before technology.
- Warm, tactile and personal rather than futuristic or neon.
- Never claim to bring an animal back to life.
- Never imply consciousness, memories, a soul, or messages from a deceased pet.
- Support both living pets and old photographs without requiring a user to label a pet as living or deceased.
- Avoid manipulative grief language.

## Typography

- Display: Fraunces (editorial serif)
- UI/body: DM Sans
- Fallbacks: Georgia / system-ui

## Visual language

**Old photograph × warm home × modern product.**

Use natural light, warm rooms, cream fabrics, worn wood, subtle brass and pet-level camera angles. Pets should provide much of the colour. Avoid neon gradients, cyberpunk AI imagery, paw-print overload, grief-stock photography and funeral-black pages.

## Website structure

The web surface uses a calm modular layout inspired by the structural principles of the Hollow wellness template: generous whitespace, modular sections, editorial typography, rounded photography and restrained motion. WornTag uses original copy, assets and brand styling.

Core pages:

- Home
- How it works
- FAQ
- Our story
- Stories / future SEO journal
- Privacy and terms (pre-launch notices until service-specific versions are ready)
