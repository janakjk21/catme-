This notice covers the WornTag website and the optional beta invite list. It is not the final privacy policy for a future app or photo-generation service.

Current status: the beta page shows whether sign-ups are open. When open, it collects only an email address after a separate, unticked consent choice. This site does not accept pet photos, create accounts, or take payments.

Information this website receives

When you visit a website, the hosting provider and network services may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser details and the page requested to deliver and protect the site. WornTag has not added a website analytics or advertising tracker to this site. The hosting provider’s operational logs are subject to its own service terms and retention settings.

The beta invite list

If sign-ups are open and you choose the unticked consent box, WornTag uses your email address only to send beta access and availability updates. The list also records when consent was given and which version of the consent wording appeared. We do not ask for your name, pet photo, or payment details, and we do not use this address for another company’s marketing. The list is stored in WornTag’s Railway-hosted database. Joining does not guarantee beta access.

You can remove an address at any time on the Beta page. At removal, the address is deleted from the invite list. Addresses remaining when the beta invitation period closes will be deleted within 30 days of closure.

Photographs and generated companions

The website does not accept photographs and does not create a companion. Before those features become available, we will publish the responsible organisation’s contact details, the purposes and legal basis for processing, service providers, international transfers, retention periods, deletion process and the rights available to you.

Cookies, local storage and fonts

This website does not currently set WornTag analytics or advertising cookies, and it does not use local storage for a WornTag account. The page requests typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to retrieve them and shares ordinary connection information such as its network address. Essential technical storage used by the hosting or security provider may also be outside the website’s control.

Children

This website is an informational pre-launch site and is not designed to collect information from children. No child should submit personal details or photographs here.

Changes and contact

This notice will be expanded before any account or photo feature launches. A public privacy contact address has not yet been published; for beta-list removal, use the self-service option on the Beta page.
