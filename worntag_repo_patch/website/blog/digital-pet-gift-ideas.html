Digital Pet Gift Ideas for Cat and Dog Owners

By the WornTag team · Published 2 October 2026

A digital pet companion is a gift idea for cat and dog owners who already have a drawer full of photo frames. WornTag is being built to turn a favourite photo into a 3D companion you can see, call and play with, but gifting is not available yet.

This list covers digital and non-digital pet gifts, what each one is good for, and an honest note on where WornTag stands today.

What are good digital pet gift ideas?

The best digital pet gifts turn a real photo of the owner’s own pet into something they can use.

  • A digital pet companion made from their pet’s photo (WornTag is planned for this, see below).
  • A shared online album of the pet’s best photos.
  • A digital portrait or illustration made from a photo.
  • A photo-based screensaver or phone wallpaper set.

Why give a digital gift to a pet owner?

Pet owners are numerous, and many already have prints and frames.

The American Pet Products Association (APPA) reports that 95 million US households own a pet. With that many pet photos in circulation, a gift that does something new with one stands out more than another frame.

How do digital and physical pet gifts compare?

Physical gifts last on a shelf, while digital gifts can move and be shared.

Can you gift a WornTag today?

No, you cannot gift WornTag yet, because the app and gifting options are not available.

A personalised pet companion may suit a gift, but WornTag has not announced a purchase route, pricing or supported phones. The beta list is open if you want to hear when testing begins. Joining does not guarantee a place and does not ask you to pay.

Is a digital pet gift right for someone grieving?

It can be, if you choose gently and set honest expectations.

  • A digital likeness is not the animal and does not hold memories or personality.
  • Offer it as a keepsake, never as a replacement.
  • Let the person choose the photo.

WornTag is designed with these limits in mind. It does not bring a pet back and does not claim to speak for one.

What should you check before you buy any digital pet gift?

Check what happens to the photo, what the gift costs and which devices it works on.

  • How long the photo is kept, and how to delete it.
  • Whether the price is one-off or recurring.
  • Which phones are supported.
