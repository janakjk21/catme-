# Current Durable Decisions

Older room-era decisions are intentionally not repeated here; Git history and `context/archive/pre-garden/` retain engineering context. They do not define current product behavior.

## 2026-09-29 — Garden companion direction

The owner avatar is the controllable lead and the personalized cat follows. Use the approved character scale and slightly elevated trailing camera. The core play loop is walking together and throwing one ball for the cat to chase. Existing HomeRoom code and local app data remain intact until a migration is planned; do not extend that scene as the product direction.

## 2026-09-29 — First session begins with a gentle reunion

After pair setup, the cat playfully hides nearby. The player follows subtle clues and finds it in a close-up greeting; then movement and ball play are introduced. Keep this search brief, accessible, skippable, safe, and non-punitive. Optional deeper separation/remembrance stories come later and must be user-selected and handled gently.

## 2026-09-29 — Story and bonding scope

First make garden movement and ball play enjoyable. Later consider short optional chapters and small reactions such as checking back, catching up, noticing garden details, and responding to a gentle interaction. Use animation/audio only when the loaded rigs support them. Avoid dialogue-heavy prompts, guilt, permanent loss, or daily-pressure systems.

## 2026-09-29 — Photo pipelines require production hardening

The current garden prototype has recorded direct Tripo cat and Meshy human calls with local ignored Unity credentials and phone-local asset storage. This is a private prototype arrangement, not a distributable architecture. Before public distribution, remove bundled keys/direct calls and use authenticated HTTPS jobs, with consent, deletion, cost controls, and retry/fallback behavior. Do not spend provider credits for routine testing.

## 2026-09-29 — Mobile evidence boundary

Editor smoke checks and successful builds do not prove device comfort. Verify camera, character scale, touch controls, sound, safe areas, performance, save/reload, and the first-session journey on a physical phone before closing a mobile milestone.
