# Economy and Shop Contract

## Principles

- The relationship with a cat is the product. Purchases must not exploit hunger, absence, grief, or attachment.
- Basic care remains available without payment or grinding.
- Bond is earned through interaction and cannot be purchased.
- Start with one soft earned token and direct real-money Studio Cat purchases.
- Do not add paid token packs, subscriptions, advertising, or additional currencies before product validation.

## Studio Cats

- Sell each Studio Cat as a clear one-time purchase.
- The player permanently owns purchased cats.
- Each has a realistic appearance, personality, animation set, favorite items, and one signature action.
- The shop preview shows the actual cat performing its signature action before purchase.
- Players may own several cats but keep one active in the first room.
- Personalized cats retain all core care and toy interactions even when Studio Cats have additional authored actions.

## Shop access

The shop exists as a physical catalog or tablet inside the room. Tapping it opens a focused shop view. Leaving the shop returns the player to the same room and cat state.

Do not cover the ambient room with a permanent large Shop button.

## Earned tokens

Award tokens through:

- completed play sessions
- new behavior discoveries
- candid and player-created memories
- bond-stage milestones
- occasional gifts from the cat

Avoid mandatory daily streaks and repetitive action farming. Apply cooldowns and diminishing rewards to repeated interactions.

## Items

- Basic food is always available.
- Special foods are consumable and reveal preferences or provide modest mood and bond bonuses.
- Toys and decorations are permanent unlocks.
- Different cats have different food and toy preferences.
- Small toys and decorations can be placed by the player in valid authored room locations.
- Functional objects publish interaction points and navigation clearance requirements.

## Validation questions

Before adding an item, confirm that it creates a visible behavior, meaningful room change, preference discovery, or useful expression. Do not add inventory whose only purpose is increasing a number.
