## 3. AI & photo policy

**What happens to your photos.** When you make a pet or a 3D you, your photo is sent to our server and then to companies that help us:

1. **OpenAI** checks the photo is safe and shows the right thing (a cat, a dog, or one person).
2. **Tripo** receives the pet photo, redraws it with its `chat_image_2` (ChatGPT image) step, and makes the 3D pet and its walk. **Meshy** receives the cropped selfie, makes an image reference with its `gpt-image-2` option, and makes the 3D you and its walk.

Details, and how long things are kept, are in the [Privacy Policy](https://worntag.com/privacy).

**Only upload photos you are allowed to use.**
- A photo of **your own pet**, and a **selfie of you**. The selfie is taken with the front camera in the app.
- Do not upload photos of other people, of children, or photos you do not have the right to use.
- Do not upload nudity, sexual content, violence, gore, hateful or illegal content, or anything that harms an animal.

**What we refuse.** Photos are checked before anything is made. A photo that is unsafe, does not show a cat or a dog (or one person, for a selfie), shows several, or is too dark or blurry is refused and deleted straight away. A refused or failed creation does not use up a free creation. We may suspend accounts that keep uploading content against these rules.

**AI results.** Your 3D characters are made by AI. They are an interpretation of your photo and may not look exactly like your pet or you. Shapes, colours and movement can be imperfect. A 3D pet in WornTag is a playful companion; it is not a replacement for a real animal.

**Training.** We do not train AI models on your photos. OpenAI does not train on data sent through its API by default. Meshy's terms allow training on non-enterprise inputs and outputs unless a separate agreement changes that; its current help page says it does not presently train on uploaded images, but does not make that a permanent promise. Provider copies and training rules are described in the Privacy Policy.
