**Description**

Bring a photo of your cat or dog into a quiet garden you can explore together. WornTag makes a 3D companion from your pet's photo and a walking 3D you from a selfie. Call your pet, play with a ball, follow the path to the river, and return to a house with room for your own photos.

Start with a guest account. A photo check runs before generation, and the app shows progress while your companion is made. You can delete your uploaded photos or your account from the Account menu.

3D creation requires an internet connection and may take several minutes. Results vary with the quality of the source photo. Dog generation must remain disabled in the listing until the production dog pipeline has been tested and enabled.
